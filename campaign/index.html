LDT / 01 — The story

A stable life is part of the work.

This campaign supports more than a degree. It creates the conditions to complete clinical-psychology training, sustain independent living, and build a responsible practice that connects people with support beyond the therapy room.

Its focus is trauma-informed research, culturally responsive care, and clear navigation toward suitable legal, social, and community resources. The project does not replace emergency, medical, legal, or social services; it helps create better routes toward them.

LDT / 02 — The digital book

A book made to be opened, not merely displayed.

The Justice of Our Society is a living digital reading room: Fire Horse red, gold signal lines, terminal clarity, and a human centre.

It brings together Qur’anic ethics, Abrahamic history, comparative spiritual traditions, and a civic commitment to justice. It does not claim that religions are identical, nor that one people owns another’s faith. It asks readers to meet difference without dehumanising anyone.

LDT / 03 — What support unlocks

A transparent, practical release plan.

Education & independence

Support protects the time and stability needed to complete the M.Sc. track and sustain daily life while research and writing move forward.

  • Final academic requirements and thesis work
  • Rent, food, transport, and basic independence
  • Ethical fieldwork, research tools, and analysis

Book & public-care edition

Support turns a manuscript into a durable, accessible work rather than a private file.

  • Source review, editing, and fact-checking
  • Seven-language translation and cultural review
  • Digital design, accessibility, and a free public preview

LDT editorial promise: distinguish faith from coercion, criticism of policies from hatred of people, evidence from speculation, and accountability from public humiliation.
