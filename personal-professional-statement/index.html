PERSONAL & PROFESSIONAL STATEMENT

Boundaries · Integrity · Consent · Human Dignity

Based in an Arab city and addressing an international audience, I am bringing together my personal and professional principles in one statement. My focus is psychology, education, research, music, and constructive collaboration. I seek to work, study, create, maintain meaningful relationships, and live peacefully with dignity and privacy.

PROFESSIONAL WORK & FAIR COMPENSATION

I no longer accept unpaid professional work, consultation, or exchanges of expertise in psychology, sociology, feminist studies, law, or medicine. Any engagement involving my time, knowledge, creative work, or professional connections requires agreed compensation, a defined scope, and ethical terms. All services must remain within my qualifications and professional remit.

My personal availability, previous assistance, or relationship with someone does not establish an ongoing obligation to provide work, introductions, resources, or support. Partnerships and collaborations require clear, freely agreed terms.

PRIVACY & CONFIDENTIALITY

I am committed to protecting therapeutic confidentiality, subject to valid consent and applicable legal, professional, or safeguarding duties. Reputational disagreements alone do not justify unnecessary disclosure of confidential information.

I ask that my personal, family, health, and professional information be treated respectfully. Please do not forward private correspondence, disclose sensitive information, or publish accounts of my private life without appropriate authorization or a specific applicable obligation.

Concerns about my teaching, therapeutic work, or other professional activities should be raised factually, respectfully, and through appropriate channels.

PERSONAL RELATIONSHIPS & INDEPENDENCE

My relationship with myself, my personal connections, and my care for animals and other living beings are deeply personal. Please respect the distinctions between my private life, friendships, family relationships, academic connections, and professional work.

Being seen with someone does not mean I endorse their conduct, share their beliefs, or authorize them to represent me. Supporting someone’s rehabilitation never means approving further wrongdoing. No person’s past, associations, disagreements, or experiences of harm justify mistreating them—or anyone else.

I speak for myself. Personal, family, academic, professional, religious, or social connections should not be presented as permission to represent me. Please obtain my express permission before using my name, image, voice, music, or other work for promotion, endorsement, or representation.

CONSENT & ACCESS TO MY LIFE

My consent must be conscious, informed, freely given, specific, and ongoing. Silence, distress, familiarity, previous cooperation, incomplete information, or assumptions about my intentions do not constitute permission.

Please identify yourself and your purpose when approaching me. Do not contact others, arrange commitments, or claim to protect or speak for me without my express authorization. Access to my time, work, home, relationships, and private life requires my agreement.

Past closeness does not create permanent access. Please respect requests for distance or no further contact, including through intermediaries, and keep my family outside personal or professional disagreements.

LOYALTY & ACCOUNTABILITY

For me, loyalty means honesty, respect, accountability, and protection from harm. It never requires silence about abuse, concealment of wrongdoing, or surrendering personal boundaries.

I welcome relationships grounded in goodwill and respectful conduct. Where those conditions are absent, I ask for distance. Good intentions must be reflected in behaviour that respects my boundaries and the dignity of others.

PROTECTION FROM ABUSE & EXPLOITATION

My position on Palestine, and everywhere else, is unequivocal: I reject colonial domination, dispossession, kidnapping, murder, sexual violence, trafficking, and exploitation. Children, women, and men deserve protection regardless of religion, nationality, family background, or social position.

I do not participate in, authorize, endorse, or tolerate such conduct. My name, professional identity, or relationships must never be used to suggest otherwise.

No appeal to security, religion, political loyalty, family honour, or institutional authority can ethically excuse abuse. This principle applies in prisons, neighbourhoods, households, workplaces, and services entrusted with protecting families.

I reject sexual exploitation and trafficking, including the conduct exposed in the Epstein cases. That reference expresses my opposition to abuse; it does not allege an undisclosed connection between those cases and any person or institution in Palestine.

Sexual activity or favours must never be offered or expected as payment, gratitude, or a condition of services, referrals, employment, collaboration, or professional opportunities.

PROPERTY, AUTHORITY & FAIR PROCESS

I oppose using bureaucratic access, historical legal arrangements, or proceedings conducted in someone’s absence to facilitate dispossession or evade accountability. Particular land claims require examination of records and evidence. Violence against people can never provide moral justification for taking their property.

This public statement does not grant anyone financial, contractual, or legal authority to act on my behalf.

I reject cultures of shaming, intimidation, retaliation, and enforced silence. People who report harm deserve a fair hearing, protection of privacy, and a responsible process. Concerns should be addressed through evidence and appropriate channels.

FAITH & RESPECT FOR DIFFERENCE

Judaism, Christianity, Islam, and other beliefs must never be invoked to excuse exploitation or deny another person’s dignity. Responsibility belongs to individuals and institutions according to their conduct and the evidence, rather than to entire religious communities.

SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY & PERSONAL CHOICES

As an educator, student, mental health professional, and someone engaged with feminist thought, I recognize the responsibility that comes with social influence. My personal choices are not professional recommendations or invitations for others to imitate them.

I choose not to drink alcohol and expect that decision to be respected. Questions about anyone’s substance use or recovery should be approached with dignity, without coercion, humiliation, or exploitation. Respecting someone’s choices does not require others to participate in them.

Having grown up across different societies, including Palestine during the Second Intifada, I understand that relationships formed amid conflict, emergencies, and hardship can be complex. An encounter viewed from a distance rarely tells the whole story. Please avoid speculation and judgments based solely on proximity or association.

My beliefs, affiliations, and personal relationships are mine to disclose and explain. I reject discrimination, class prejudice, exclusion, and interference with my ability to work, study, maintain relationships, or live peacefully.

PEACEFUL INTENTIONS & FUTURE ENGAGEMENT

My intentions are peaceful. I seek truth, safety, privacy, and accountability. If harm occurs, I will seek protection through appropriate lawful channels.

My guiding conviction is this: “If we can understand and end crime in Palestine, we can contribute to understanding and preventing it everywhere.”

I regret any unintended hurt or misunderstanding in previous communication and wish to move forward with goodwill. For legitimate personal or professional matters, contact me directly through my established communication channels. Respectful communication, clear agreements, and freely given consent are the basis on which I choose to engage.

This statement clarifies my principles and boundaries. It makes no allegation against an unnamed person or group and does not imply any undisclosed affiliation or dispute.

Hadi Omar
