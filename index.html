The Audio Project: Congolese Drums & Global South Unity

Central Premise: Historically in Central Africa, Congolese drums communicated nationwide phenomena across vast distances. This project places Congolese drums at the center, weaving musical elements from over 50 continental African nations into unified instrumental tracks.

Traced from Felakutism, 70s dancehall, and early hip-hop’s core principles of Peace, Love, Unity, and Having Fun, this album operates as a musical march toward Palestine and global solidarity.

Clinical & Interdisciplinary Foundation: Developed alongside Master's thesis work on non-triggering PTSD models (PCTSS), Solfeggio frequencies, legal frameworks, and feminist studies. By providing instrumental, non-verbal sound therapy, this music allows clinical guidance beyond the physical therapy room, fostering cross-cultural resilience, anti-colonial non-conformity, and faith-based unity.

Fundraiser Impact Study: Solving the Clinical Gap

The Theoretical Problem: Western diagnostic models (e.g., DSM-5 PTSD) assume trauma is a past event that has ended. For populations under ongoing conflict or chronic insecurity, danger is continuous. Standard diagnostic scales often misdiagnose normal survival adaptations as pathology or force participants to re-live traumatic memories.

The Solution (PCTSS & GS-CTSS): This fundraiser supports the field administration, validation, and open-access publication of two non-triggering scales evaluating 9 critical domains:

  • Daily Continuous Trauma & Threat Exposure
  • Media Retraumatization & Digital Warfare
  • Normalization of Survival & Emotional Exhaustion
  • Identity & Sociopolitical Pressure
  • Economic, Civil, & Mobility Stress
  • Neurophysiological Stress & Autonomic Dysregulation
  • Functional Dissociation & Survival Adaptation
  • Emotional Numbing & Conscious Community
  • Collective Survival & Existential Anxiety
