Independent peer-review standards

Peer-review access is limited to reviewers with independently verified, relevant academic qualifications (normally an accredited degree in the discipline or closely related area), an appropriate record of subject expertise, and a documented commitment to research ethics. Short attendance courses alone do not meet that qualification threshold. Verification is conducted directly with the awarding institution or other reliable credential source.

Reviewers must disclose conflicts of interest, safeguard confidential material, use humane and consent-based research approaches, and follow non-discrimination and professional integrity standards. Unethical animal research, harassment, coercive practices, fabricated qualifications or discriminatory conduct are incompatible with reviewer eligibility. Decisions are made on conduct, relevant expertise and verifiable qualifications—not nationality, religion, identity, or legitimate scholarly disagreement. Coursework and research are not represented as peer-reviewed unless review has actually occurred.

Prospective reviewers: request the confidential reviewer application procedure. Do not send identity documents or student records to an unsecured public link. Credentials will be requested only through an approved secure submission channel.
