From Al-Ḥamrāʾ to the Alhambra: language, memory and the Mediterranean.

Editorial essay

Different languages. Shared histories.

Across centuries, languages have carried traces of cultural exchange, migration, scholarship, commerce and everyday life.

This project explores Arabic linguistic heritage already present within Spanish, Catalan and Asturian, focusing on historically established words rather than inventing a new language.

A guiding principle

Preserved identities.

The aim is not to erase cultural differences, but to understand historical relationships that remain visible in language today.
