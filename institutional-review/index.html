For a bank, employer or institutional reviewer

To examine original evidence, request a scoped verification packet: professional CV and portfolio; selected essays and original work; relevant educational credentials; business proposal; and authorized music samples or licensing documentation. Confidential documents should be transferred through an institution-approved secure portal, not by public GitHub link or unsolicited email attachment.

  1. Identify the reviewing institution and purpose of assessment.
  2. Specify the evidence needed and the approved secure submission channel.
  3. Independently verify credentials with issuing institutions and check any regulated professional permissions.
  4. Arrange any commercial terms, intellectual-property permissions and payments through written agreements and verified financial channels.
